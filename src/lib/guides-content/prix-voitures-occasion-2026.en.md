## Factors that raise or lower the price

- **Year of first use**: a car that entered service earlier sells for less, even with the same model year.
- **Model popularity and spare parts**: common models (Symbol, Sandero, Clio) have available, affordable parts — they hold their price.
- **Fuel**: diesel and LPG (Sirgaz) equipped cars are more in demand and more expensive.
- **Scarcity of new cars**: the few new cars available push all used-car prices up.

## Final advice

The median price is a starting point for negotiation, not a final price — inspect the car yourself, or with a trusted mechanic, before any agreement.
