# Used car prices in Algeria 2026

Knowing the true market price is your best weapon — as a seller to set a fair price, as a buyer to negotiate with confidence. This guide presents prices calculated from real listings published on Dlala, updated continuously.

> These prices are taken from listings published on the Dlala website. They are updated continuously with every new listing.

## How to read these prices?

- **Median price**: the middle price of published listings — half of the listings are cheaper, the other half more expensive. It is barely affected by outlier prices.
- **Price range**: it spans from the first quartile (p25) to the third quartile (p75) — half of the listings fall within this range.
- **Number of listings**: the higher it is, the more reliable the median price. No model is shown with fewer than 5 listings.
- Prices in Algerian dinars (DZD), reflecting the current market, with no official appraisal value.

## Factors that raise or lower the price

- **Mileage**: each additional 50,000 km bracket usually lowers the price noticeably.
- **Gearbox**: automatic usually costs more than manual for the same model.
- **Fuel**: diesel holds its value better in Algeria thanks to low fuel prices.
- **General condition**: documented service history, new tires, no accidents — all of this pulls the price toward the top of the range.
- **Wilaya**: prices in large wilayas (Algiers, Oran, Constantine) are often slightly higher due to demand.

## Final advice

Use these prices as a starting point for negotiation, not as an imposed final price. Always inspect the vehicle before any agreement — a car at the "right" price but in poor condition ends up costing more than a pricier car in excellent condition.
